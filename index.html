A calmer routine for your baby's feeds, sleep and nappies

Bright Birth is an iPhone app that helps parents and carers set up a simple daily system and work toward a routine, from newborn to toddler.

Coming soon to the App Store

  • Log the day in a few tapsFeeds, sleep and nappies, with one button for asleep and awake.
  • Work toward a routineSet a bedtime and a wake-up time, and see how the days are settling.
  • Share with your familyEveryone who looks after your baby sees the same day.

Bright Birth follows standard guidelines. They are a starting point, not absolute rules. Every child is different, so take anything that worries you to your own paediatrician. The app never diagnoses and never gives medication doses.
